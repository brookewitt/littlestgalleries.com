Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Littlest Galleries collects when you use this website or commission a piece, and how it's used. Littlest Galleries is a small studio in London run by me, Brooke, and I'm responsible for your information. Contact me at brooke@littlestgalleries.com.

What I collect

  • From the commission form: your name, email address, chosen size and delivery week, how you found me, and anything you tell me about your child and their artwork.
  • When you pay: payments are handled by my bank, Tide, on its secure checkout. Tide collects your name, email address, delivery address and card details. I never see your full card details.
  • When we email: anything you choose to send me, including photos.

How I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, make and deliver your commission, and take payment. This is necessary to carry out our agreement.
  • To understand how people find my studio (from the "How did you find me?" question), which helps me run my business. This is in my legitimate interests.
  • I don't send marketing emails unless you've asked to receive them, and I never sell your information.

Who I share it with

  • Formspree, which delivers the commission form to my inbox. It may process data outside the UK, with appropriate safeguards.
  • Tide, which processes payments.
  • Couriers, who need your name and address to deliver your piece.
  • My email provider, which stores our correspondence.

How long I keep it

I keep records of commissions and payments for 6 years, as UK law requires for business accounts. If an enquiry doesn't become a commission, I delete it within 12 months.

Cookies and this website

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser shares its IP address with Google to load them. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, or ask me to correct or delete it, by emailing me. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
