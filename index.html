The Process

Simple, personal, beautiful

Every piece is made by hand, with care. Here's how it works — from your child's scribbles to a finished artwork you'll hang with pride.

1. Gather & Send

Collect your child's drawings, paintings and handprints and post them to my London studio. I'll include as many pieces as I can and return any I don't use.

2. Design & Create

I hand-compose and collage your piece, picking out the most wonderful moments. No two are ever the same.

3. Delivered & Displayed

Your piece arrives professionally framed in light oak, ready to hang and treasure for years to come.

FAQ

Questions answered

What kind of artwork can I send?
Any artwork your child has created — paintings, drawings, crayon scribbles, watercolours, felt-tip creations, and mixed media all work beautifully. The more varied the pieces, the richer the final collage. As a guide, send at least 40 pieces for a 24×30, 50 for a 30×40 and 60 for a 36×48.
How do I send the artwork to you?
Once your 75% deposit has been received, I'll email you my London studio address and instructions on how to send your child's artwork.
How does payment work?
A 75% deposit secures your delivery week. Once it's received, I'll email you my studio address and where to send the artwork. The remaining 25% is due before your finished piece is dispatched. Please note the deposit becomes non-refundable once your artwork arrives at my studio.
How long does it take?
Each commission is booked into a delivery week. You can see which weeks are open in the availability calendar in the Commission a Piece section and choose one when you enquire. Please post your artwork so it reaches me at least 2 weeks before your delivery week. Delivery across the UK is fully insured and included in the price.
What sizes are available and what do they cost?
I offer three sizes: 24×30" (£640), 30×40" (£835) and 36×48" (£1,010). All prices include professional framing in a light-oak floating frame and insured UK delivery. Unframed options are also available. There are no hidden costs.
Is this a good gift idea?
It's one of the most personal gifts you can give — and one that lasts a lifetime. Customers have commissioned pieces as Christmas gifts, birthday presents, and Mother's Day gifts for grandparents. Every piece is completely unique because it's made from your child's own artwork.
Do you ship outside the UK?
Currently I specialise in UK commissions, as this keeps delivery safe and insured. If you're based outside the UK and would like to commission a piece, please get in touch and I'll do my best to help.
Can I commission a piece using artwork from more than one child?
Absolutely — sibling collages are some of my favourites. I can blend artwork from two or more children into one piece, making it an even more special family heirloom.
