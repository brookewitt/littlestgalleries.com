About Littlest Galleries

Hi, I'm Brooke

I'm an American living in London, and I have two little girls who light up my world.

I couldn't bear to throw away their artwork. So I started turning those beautiful piles into collages: one framed piece, made from their own drawings, paintings and handprints, that we could hang on the wall and enjoy every day.

Now I make them for families across the UK through Littlest Galleries. Every collage is made by hand in my London studio from your child's own artwork, so no two are ever the same.

Brooke

How I work

Made slowly, made by hand

Just two commissions a week

I keep my numbers small on purpose, so every piece gets my full attention from the first sort to the final frame.

Every piece looked at

I go through everything you send. My goal is to include as many of your child's pieces as possible, and any artwork I don't use comes back to you with your finished collage.

Framed and delivered

Framed pieces arrive in a light oak floating frame, ready to hang. Every price includes insured UK delivery, with no hidden extras.

Siblings welcome

Sibling collages are some of my favourites. I can blend artwork from two or more children into one piece, or make a matching pair.
