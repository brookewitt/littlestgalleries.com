Terms & Conditions

Last updated 2 November 2026

These terms apply when you book a bespoke collage commission from Littlest Galleries, a small studio in London run by me, Brooke. If you have any questions, email me at brooke@littlestgalleries.com.

Your commission

Every piece is made by hand from your child's own artwork, so no two are the same. I compose each collage using my own artistic judgement. If there are particular pieces you'd love to see included or featured, tell me when you book and I'll do my best to include them.

Booking and your deposit

  • A 75% deposit secures your chosen delivery week. Your booking is confirmed once the deposit has been received.
  • Delivery weeks are limited and allocated on a first come, first served basis.
  • Prices are in pounds sterling and include professional framing (for framed pieces) and insured UK delivery.

Sending your artwork

  • Once your deposit is received, I'll email you my studio address and instructions on how to send the artwork.
  • Please post it so that it reaches me at least 2 weeks before your delivery week. If it arrives later, your delivery week may need to move.
  • My goal is to include as many of your child's pieces as possible in your collage. Any artwork I don't use is returned to you with your finished piece.

The remaining balance

The remaining 25% is due before your finished piece is dispatched. I'll send you a secure payment link when your piece is ready, and dispatch it once the balance has been paid.

Cancellations and refunds

  • If you cancel before your artwork arrives at my studio, I'll refund your deposit in full.
  • Once your artwork has arrived at my studio, the deposit is non-refundable, as work on your piece begins.

Delivery

I aim to deliver your piece during your booked week. Occasionally couriers cause delays outside my control; if that happens, I'll keep you updated. Insured delivery within the UK is included. For delivery outside the UK, please get in touch before booking.

Photos of your piece

I may photograph finished pieces to share on my website and social media. I never share your child's name or photos of your child without your permission. If you'd rather I didn't share your piece at all, just let me know when you book.
